Privacy Policy — Bujji Badi

Last Updated: November 2026

Bujji Badi ("the App") is a learning app for young children, developed and maintained by SP Future AI. Your child's privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Bujji Badi handles information.

1. No Personal Data Collected

Bujji Badi does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No cloud sync of personal data
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location

2. What Data Is Stored On The Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Settings (language, sound options, home background)
  • Favourite activities and which letters/categories were opened (stars)
  • Simple play statistics shown to the parent in the "Parent Dashboard" (minutes per day, which games and categories were opened)
  • Whether ads were removed (after the optional one-time purchase)

This data is never sent anywhere and can be deleted at any time from the dashboard ("Reset dashboard data") or by uninstalling the App.

3. Learning Content Updates

The App may download learning content updates (new words, pictures) from our content server. No personal data is sent when this happens — only anonymous requests for the content files themselves.

4. Advertisements

The free version may show banner and occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are configured specifically for children:

  • Child-directed treatment and under-age-of-consent flag
  • Maximum content rating "G"
  • Non-personalised ads only — no behavioural targeting
  • The Android advertising ID (AD_ID) is not requested or used

Ads are not shown after the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase.

5. In-App Purchases

The optional one-time purchase to remove ads is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

6. Parental Gate

Settings, ad removal, purchases, and any external links are placed behind a simple "grown-ups only" check (parental gate) so that young children cannot access them by accident.

7. Children's Privacy

Bujji Badi is designed for children and is built to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

Other than Google AdMob (for non-personalised, child-directed ads) and Google Play Billing (for the optional purchase), the app does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or remote servers.

9. Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and the date above will change.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns, please mention the app name in the subject — for example: "Bujji Badi - Question about ads".
